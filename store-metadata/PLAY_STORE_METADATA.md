## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Seeding is your private, judgment-free companion for tracking recovery and building healthier habits. All your data stays on your device—no cloud sync, no accounts, no tracking.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• 100% local storage - your data never leaves your device
• No account required - start tracking immediately
• No cloud sync or third-party analytics
• Optional biometric app lock for extra security
• Export your data anytime in Excel or CSV format

📊 TRACK YOUR JOURNEY
• Real-time progress timer showing days, hours, and minutes
• Growth stages that evolve as you progress (Seed to Tree)
• Log healthy activities like exercise, meditation, and social connection
• Track relapses with privacy-focused tags and notes
• Create custom activity categories with emojis

📈 INSIGHTS & ANALYTICS
• Resistance ratio showing your success rate
• Weekly and monthly pattern analysis
• Comparative stats (weekly, monthly, 30-day trends)
• Activity effectiveness tracking
• Identify triggers and patterns over time

🏆 ACHIEVEMENTS & MOTIVATION
• 14 time-based milestones (5 minutes to 1 year)
• Activity-based achievement badges
• Real-time celebration when you unlock milestones
• Track your best streak and total attempts
• Stoic wisdom and motivational quotes

⚡ HELPFUL FEATURES
• Quick action cards for one-tap activity logging
• Emergency support resources when you need them
• Daily reminders and milestone notifications
• Random motivational notifications throughout the day
• Dark mode with smooth transitions
• Accessibility features including reduced motion support

🧠 EDUCATIONAL CONTENT
• Learn about dopamine and recovery science
• Understand the psychology of habit formation
• Evidence-based recovery principles
• In-app guides and tutorials

💯 NO PREMIUM FEATURES
Everything is free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads. Just a tool to help you grow.

Seeding respects your privacy and your journey. Whether you're working on breaking a habit or building new ones, we're here to support you—without judgment, without tracking, and without compromising your data.

Your growth matters. Start planting your seeds today.

## Testing Instructions for Google Review Team

**How to Test:**
1. Install and open the app
2. Complete the 3-step onboarding tutorial
3. Tap "Track Your Growth" to log a healthy activity
4. Select activity categories (Exercise, Meditation, etc.)
5. Add optional notes and tap Save
6. View the Home screen to see updated stats
7. Navigate to History tab - switch between List and Calendar views
8. Open Achievements tab - view Milestones and Badges
9. Go to Settings:
   - Try theme switching (Light/Dark)
   - Enable notifications (requires permission)
   - Try App Lock (requires device biometric setup)
   - Test Export to Excel feature
10. Tap emergency help button (!) in top-right of Home screen

**Key Privacy Notes:**
- No network calls for user data collection
- All data stored in local SQLite database
- No analytics or tracking SDKs
- No third-party data sharing
- Biometric and notification permissions are optional
