# Privacy Policy for Seeding

**Last Updated:** January 13, 2026

**Effective Date:** January 13, 2026

---

## Introduction

Welcome to Seeding ("we," "our," or "the app"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your information.

**The Short Version:** Seeding stores all your data locally on your device. We don't collect, transmit, or share any of your personal information. Ever.

---

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Seeding stores the following information **exclusively on your device** in an encrypted local database:

- **Recovery Journey Data**
  - Journey start date and time
  - Relapse records (timestamp, optional notes, optional tags)
  - Activity logs (timestamp, categories, optional notes)
  - Progress statistics and streaks

- **App Settings**
  - Theme preference (light/dark mode)
  - Notification settings and preferences
  - App lock biometric settings
  - Custom activity tags created by you

- **Achievement Progress**
  - Unlocked milestones and badges
  - Achievement timestamps

**Important:** This data is stored using SQLite database on your device and is never transmitted to any external servers, cloud services, or third parties.

### Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:
- ❌ Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Device identifiers for tracking
- ❌ Usage analytics or telemetry
- ❌ Crash reports (unless you manually share them)
- ❌ Any data about your recovery journey or activities
- ❌ Advertising identifiers
- ❌ Social media information
- ❌ Contacts or photos

---

## How We Use Your Information

Since all data stays on your device:
- ✅ We display your journey statistics and progress
- ✅ We calculate insights and patterns from your local data
- ✅ We send local notifications (if you enable them)
- ✅ We unlock achievements based on your progress

**We do not:**
- ❌ Access your data remotely
- ❌ Share your data with anyone
- ❌ Use your data for advertising
- ❌ Sell your data to third parties
- ❌ Train AI models on your data
- ❌ Create user profiles

---

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

**We do not share any data with third parties. Period.**

Seeding does not integrate with:
- Analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Social media platforms
- Cloud storage services
- Data brokers

The only time your data leaves your device is when **you** explicitly choose to export it using the "Export to Excel" or "Export to CSV" features in Settings.

---

## Data Security

### Local Storage
All data is stored securely on your device using:
- **SQLite Database:** Industry-standard local database
- **Expo Secure Store:** Encrypted storage for sensitive settings
- **No Cloud Sync:** Data never leaves your device

### Optional Security Features
- **Biometric App Lock:** You can enable Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint authentication to add an extra layer of protection
- **Export Encryption:** Exported files are not encrypted, so handle them securely

### Important Note
We cannot recover your data if:
- You delete the app
- You reset your device
- You use the "Reset All Data" feature in Settings

**Recommendation:** Regularly export your data as backup if it's important to you.

---

## Your Rights and Choices

Since we don't collect any data, you have complete control:

### Your Data Rights
- **Access:** All your data is accessible directly in the app
- **Export:** Export your complete data set anytime (Settings → Export)
- **Delete:** Permanently delete all data (Settings → Reset All Data)
- **Portability:** Export in standard formats (Excel, CSV) to move to other apps

### Optional Permissions

The app requests these permissions (all optional):

1. **Notifications** - To send you reminders and milestone alerts
   - You can disable this in Settings or device settings

2. **Biometric Authentication** - For app lock feature
   - Completely optional
   - Uses your device's built-in biometric system

3. **File Storage** - To save exported files
   - Only used when you manually export data

**You can revoke these permissions anytime** in your device settings.
